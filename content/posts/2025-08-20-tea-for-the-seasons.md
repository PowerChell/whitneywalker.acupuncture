In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), tea is more than a comforting beverage—it's a gentle form of medicine that can support your body through the natural rhythms of the year. Each type of tea carries its own unique energy, flavor profile, and therapeutic properties, making certain teas ideal companions for specific seasons.

Let's explore how to choose the right tea to harmonize with nature's cycles and support your health throughout the year.

## Spring & Summer – Green & White Teas

Spring calls for lightness and renewal, and our bodies crave fresh, vibrant energy. White tea, light and delicate, shines in spring with its subtle sweetness and gentle nature. Green tea is also ideal for this season of growth. Its fresh character helps clear heat from the body, supports liver function (which TCM associates strongly with spring), and promotes detoxification after the heavier foods of winter.

When summer heat arrives, green tea continues to serve us well by cooling the body and quenching thirst. Matcha offers a refreshing energy boost while maintaining cooling properties—ideal for hot afternoons when you need clarity without overheating.

## Fall – Oolong Tea

Autumn brings crisp air and increasing dryness, which can leave our lungs feeling vulnerable. This is where oolong tea truly shines. Partially oxidized and sitting between green and black tea, oolong has natural moistening qualities that help counteract seasonal dryness and nourish the lungs.

Beyond its seasonal benefits, oolong is particularly valuable for anyone exposed to respiratory stress—whether you're a runner, smoker, or live in an area with air pollution. Its moistening and detoxifying properties support lung health and respiratory function year-round.

## Winter – Black Tea

When winter's chill settles in, our bodies naturally crave warmth and grounding energy. Black tea, with its rich, full-bodied flavor and gently warming nature, is the perfect winter companion. Its robust character provides comfort during long, dark days while supporting circulation and helping your body conserve energy—exactly what winter calls for.

## Year-Round – Pu-Erh Tea

Pu-erh stands apart as a tea beneficial in every season thanks to its remarkable digestive properties. This unique fermented tea, with its earthy, complex flavor, creates beneficial compounds through its fermentation process that support gut health and help break down fats after heavier meals.

Many cultures serve a digestive aid after eating—pu-erh plays a similar role, supporting a healthy gut microbiome and helping ease the strain on your digestive system. Whether you've enjoyed a rich winter stew or a summer barbecue, a cup of pu-erh can help your body process it all more smoothly.

## Bringing It All Together

By aligning your tea choices with the seasons, you not only enjoy a delicious ritual but also harmonize your body with nature’s cycles. Whether it’s the cooling clarity of green tea in summer, the moistening qualities of oolong in fall, or the cozy warmth of black tea in winter—each cup is an invitation to balance, health, and mindfulness.
