## How Acupuncture Helps with Insomnia and Sleep Issues

Acupuncture works by inserting very fine needles at specific points on the body to regulate the nervous system, balance hormones, and calm the mind. This holistic approach addresses the root causes of sleep disturbances rather than simply masking symptoms.

Research and clinical experience show that acupuncture can:

* Reduce nighttime anxiety and racing thoughts  
* Improve sleep onset (falling asleep faster)  
* Increase total sleep duration and quality  
* Regulate circadian rhythm  
* Calm an overactive nervous system  
* Enhance daytime energy and mental clarity  
* Decrease reliance on sleep medications

## Frequently Asked Questions

*Does acupuncture work for chronic insomnia?*  
Yes. Acupuncture has been shown to help patients with chronic insomnia restore natural sleep patterns without medication. Many patients experience significant improvement in both falling asleep and staying asleep.

*How many sessions will I need?*  
Most patients notice improvements within 3-5 sessions, though chronic insomnia may require ongoing treatment. We'll create a treatment plan based on your specific sleep issues and monitor your progress closely.

*Will I feel tired after acupuncture?*  
Many patients feel deeply relaxed after treatment, and it's common to sleep especially well that night. Some people experience a pleasant sense of calm that lasts throughout the day. We often recommend scheduling treatments in the evening when possible.

*Can acupuncture help if I'm already taking sleep medication?*  
Yes. Acupuncture can be used alongside sleep medications and may help you gradually reduce your dependence on them over time. Always consult with your prescribing doctor before making changes to medications.
