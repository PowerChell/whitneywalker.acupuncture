## How Acupuncture Helps with Back Pain

Acupuncture works by inserting very fine needles at specific points on the body to stimulate healing and restore balance. This ancient practice has been shown to be particularly effective for back pain by targeting the underlying causes of discomfort.

Research and clinical experience show that acupuncture can:

* Reduce inflammation and relieve muscle tension  
* Improve blood flow to injured or strained areas  
* Stimulate the body's natural pain-relieving endorphins  
* Support long-term healing and better posture balance  
* Address nerve pain like sciatica  
* Decrease reliance on pain medications
